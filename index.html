How measurement works ↗
Can RevFirma block competing advertisers?

No. We help you compete for attention by building your own advertising presence. The platforms control their auctions, and other advertisers can still reach the same audiences.

Will everyone inside my area see my ads?

No. Delivery depends on platform location signals, eligible audiences, placement availability and the auction. We plan around your area and report the delivery achieved.

How will I know it’s working?

Review delivery, cost and frequency against the plan. Connect inquiries, bookings or purchases where measurement is available. Ad delivery alone is not proof of sales or revenue lift.

Privacy & your inquiry

RevFirma is operated by AI Sales, Inc. When you request a managed advertising plan, we receive your name, business, email, target area, selected interests and any notes you provide. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and discuss a proposed service. Please include business information relevant to your request.

The form also includes the page address and referring page, when available. Submissions are stored using Supabase and sent to our team through our email provider, Resend. On revfirma.com, the site uses Matomo and Meta measurement tools to understand visits, page interactions and completed inquiries. Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on this page. These services can receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and page activity.

For a question about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact james@revfirma.com. You can also email or call us directly to discuss an advertising plan.
