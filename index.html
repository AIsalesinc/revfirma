Before you choose

Clear answers.
Clear expectations.

What does “protect your audience” mean?

It means actively managing your advertising presence around a place that matters to your business: your messages, campaigns, delivery goals and operating evidence. RevFirma does not block someone’s device or guarantee that competing ads disappear.

Can RevFirma stop recruiters from reaching employees?

Workforce protection builds your employer’s advertising presence around the workplace and reinforces reasons to stay. It does not block recruiters, prevent contact or guarantee employee retention. We agree the employer messaging and measurement approach as part of the plan.

What does owning my digital space mean?

You set the direction for a coordinated advertising program around your locations: its messages, participating businesses, objectives and agreed spending limits. The advertising platforms continue to own and operate their inventory. Your perimeter gives you a managed presence and reporting for the delivery you purchase.

Will you reach every person inside the perimeter?

Delivery depends on platform location signals, audience eligibility, settings and available ad opportunities. A geofence is a targeting area, not a precise census or a guarantee that every person physically inside it receives an ad.

How will I know what I am receiving?

Your reporting can show campaign impressions, reported reach, frequency and paid cost. Network exposure estimates and goal progress are labeled separately. We define which customer actions can be tracked as part of your campaign plan.

How do you measure progress toward saturation?

We agree the area, surfaces and exposure goal in your plan. Goal progress compares estimated ad exposure with that target. A share of all advertising is a separate measure that requires evidence of both our delivery and the total advertising in the same scope. Your reporting identifies which measure is being shown.

Can you tell who is targeting my customers?

Reported ad sightings can show which outside advertisers were observed. Changes in our delivery and cost can flag conditions to investigate. They do not reveal another advertiser’s private audience settings or prove who a particular campaign intended to target.

What does a perimeter cost?

Pricing depends on geography, campaign duration, delivery objectives, participating businesses and operating scope. Your proposal separates the media budget and service responsibilities so you can evaluate the full cost before committing.

What do you need to get started?

Start with your business, the location or area you care about, and the result you want. We then confirm availability, campaign access, creative, destination pages, measurement and an agreed launch schedule. Request your perimeter plan.
